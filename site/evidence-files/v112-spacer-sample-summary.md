# Sampled spacer capability

Ten spacers from each labelled size were measured three times. These are batch samples, not individually tracked stock.

| Label | Typical thickness | Lowest sampled spacer average | Highest sampled spacer average | Full reading range |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.50 mm | 0.486 mm | 0.443 mm | 0.510 mm | 0.42–0.54 mm |
| 1.00 mm | 1.118 mm | 1.107 mm | 1.130 mm | 1.07–1.17 mm |
| 2.00 mm | 2.070 mm | 2.060 mm | 2.090 mm | 2.03–2.13 mm |

The unrounded sum of one typical spacer of every size is 3.674667 mm, which displays directly as 3.67 mm. The earlier complete-build measurement was 3.67 mm.

The app must still use one final measurement of the complete setup. The sample does not prove the thickness of every spacer in the larger stock.

Foil recipes remain disabled until foil-stack measurements and the practical maximum layer count are supplied.
