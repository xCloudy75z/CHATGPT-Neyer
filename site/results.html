Understand the result

Use the operating decision first.

Use the reliable operating gap for the response target selected in the study. Do not substitute the middle gap: it answers a different question.

What to do with the operating instruction

The reliable operating gap is the requested setting for the planned result. Build that setting only when it is a confirmed reachable physical setup. If the result says to pause, keep the recorded tests and review the setup rather than forcing another request.

Smaller physical gaps make Interaction more likely. As the physical gap increases, Interaction becomes less likely and No interaction becomes more likely.

Do not over-read an estimate

A value outside the tested range is an estimate, not a usable physical setting. The software documents the reviewed MATLAB implementation; it does not perform the calculation in this website.

Then read the two study summaries

Middle gap
The estimated physical gap where approximately half of similar articles may interact. It is a response summary, not a safe or reliable operating gap.
Overall variation
How much responses differ from article to article around the middle gap. Sigma is the mathematical name for overall variation. More overall variation means the change from Interaction to No interaction is spread across a wider range of gaps.

The middle-gap and overall-variation ranges are two-sided confidence ranges with lower and upper estimates. They describe uncertainty in these estimates from the recorded study data and do not turn an untested or unbuildable gap into an operating instruction.

Operating tail boundaries use one-sided confidence in the safe direction. A tail boundary is the cautious edge setting for the planned Interaction or No interaction target, so it has one relevant direction rather than an upper-and-lower estimate pair.

Keep the construction record separate

The requested gap is shown with two decimal places so the build instruction is clear. For one unchanged build, enter four or five physical readings. Their measured mean keeps the precision entered from those readings and is the fitted gap.

Each destructive test needs a newly built setup. A measured mean that differs from the request is useful information, not a reason to rewrite the original request.

Saving is deliberate

You choose the save location, see the exact CSV and HTML paths, and confirm them. Existing files are not silently replaced.
