How the method works

Read the gap result without mixing up its meanings.

Smaller physical gaps make Interaction more likely. Larger physical gaps make No interaction more likely. The tool uses your observations to estimate where that change happens.

Keep the questions separate

The middle gap describes an approximately 50/50 response point. A reliable operating gap is a separate instruction chosen for the planned response target. Neither label promises what one individual article will do.

Middle gap

The middle gap is the physical gap where approximately half of similar articles may interact. It is a summary of the study response, not an operating instruction.

Overall variation

Overall variation describes how much the response changes from article to article around the middle gap. Sigma is its mathematical name. More overall variation means the response is spread across a wider range of physical gaps.

Example, not a real study result

A middle gap of 5.39 mm means approximately half of similar articles may interact there. It does not make 5.39 mm a safe operating gap.

The example operating instruction is 2.30 mm or smaller for the planned Interaction target. That is a different answer for a different question.
