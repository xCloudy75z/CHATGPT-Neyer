Audit and corrections

Use the corrected v1.10 workflow, not the earlier v1.8 behaviour.

This table keeps original findings separate from the implemented corrections. It does not turn optional ideas or remaining limits into completed claims.

Original v1.8 problems and implemented v1.10 corrections

Areav1.8 problemv1.10 result
Stage 1Bound-aware means the search stays inside the permitted physical-gap limits. The reference sequence was reproduced, but the outward search did not fully use that rule in every branch.The bound-aware rule is used in the smaller-gap Interaction direction and checked again so the earlier problem does not return.
Stage-2 changeoverA 1.5× guessed overall-variation threshold was selected to reproduce a table, not established as the method rule.Stage 1 changes to Stage 2 at 1.0× the guessed overall variation. This is the changeover rule.
Stage-2 narrowingThe configured 0.8 narrowing factor was not applied by the decision loop.After each separated Stage-2 result, the working overall variation is multiplied by 0.8. This is a narrowing rule, not the 1.0× changeover rule.
One-way Stage 2Bisection means repeatedly halving the range between observed Interaction and No interaction. An intermediate correction could return to that earlier process.Stage 2 stays one-way and ends only after strict overlap. Strict overlap means the Interaction and No interaction ranges share more than a touching endpoint.
Confidence handlingThe core fit needed confidence checks for tail boundaries and safeguards for extreme artificial data.The middle-gap and overall-variation ranges are two-sided, with lower and upper estimates. One-sided confidence sets a cautious operating boundary in one safe direction. Extreme artificial data remain a disclosed numerical limit.
Next-gap information choiceD-optimal means choosing the next gap expected to add the most useful information. The information calculation matched the method, but the search fence and grid were engineering choices.The retained information choice is mapped to a reachable and useful physical setting.
Boundary confirmationRepeated use of a boundary could look like a failed or looping test.One confirmation is requested. A second contradiction pauses safely and preserves the record for review.
Two-decimal requestFixed decimal rounding could request a gap that the equipment could not build.The request is selected from reachable settings and displayed with two decimal places; measured readings keep their entered precision.
Useful-setting stopSome no-alternative cases could repeat a setting without a clear reason.The app chooses the nearest different reachable and useful setting, or pauses when none exists.

Source: the reviewed v1.10 delivery/Neyer_Overnight_Verification_Report.html#audit.

Implemented independent-review corrections

Final review also corrected safety fields that could fail open, prevented a 0.015 mm foil observation from being treated as usable resolution, and prevented a later boundary clamp from undoing a reachable setting.

The named corrections are described in the reviewed v1.10 delivery/Neyer_Overnight_Verification_Report.html#audit. The separate aggregate record reports the 63 completed final-review checks: audit/overnight/final-review-regressions.txt.

Optional ideas and remaining limitations

More physical measurements of spacer combinations are still optional future work. Extreme artificial confidence cases and final physical reachability remain limitations to review, not evidence that every possible setup is supported.
