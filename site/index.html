What this review site covers

This site explains the reviewed MATLAB tool. It does not run or change the calculation. It keeps the middle gap, the reliable operating gap, and overall variation separate because they answer different questions.

Requested gaps are written with two decimal places. The measured mean keeps the precision entered from the physical readings.
